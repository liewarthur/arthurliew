Where ancient stories meet future vision.

Rooted in folklore and tradition, reimagined through technology, AI, and the ambition to see what no frame has captured before.

Arthur is a Kuala Lumpur-based film director and educator — where technical craft meets cinematic instinct.

Founder of production house Blackdotpeople, his work spans commercial campaigns to short films. A member of FDAM and PROFIMA, rooted in Malaysia's creative industry.

Most recently an alumnus of the BIFAN NAFF Fantastic Film School, Arthur directs, lectures, and explores the frontier of AI-driven visual storytelling — always evolving, always story-first.
